Privacy Policy

UltraSpeed (iPhone / Apple Watch)

This policy explains how SHIFTEX, LIMITED LIABILITY COMPANY ("we") handles your information in the cycling computer app "UltraSpeed" (the "App"). If this English version and the Japanese version differ, the Japanese version prevails.

1. Summary

  • Information the App handles is stored only on your devices (iPhone and Apple Watch) and in Health.
  • The App never sends your information to our servers. We cannot collect or see your information.
  • The App contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

2. Information the App uses and why

Location

Used to measure speed, distance, and your route during a ride, and to show your position and route on the map. The App also uses location in the background so a ride keeps recording when the screen is off.

Barometric altimeter (Motion & Fitness)

Used to calculate grade and elevation gain.

Health

Rides are saved to Health as workouts with routes and heart rate, and heart rate and active calories are read to show them during a ride. Health data is never used for advertising or sold, and we never send it anywhere outside your devices, including iCloud.

3. Where data is stored

  • Ride records (date and time, distance, speed, elevation, heart rate, route) are stored inside the App on your device.
  • Workouts, routes, and heart rate are also saved to Health if you allow it.
  • Rides recorded on Apple Watch are sent directly to your paired iPhone using Apple's WatchConnectivity. They do not pass through our servers.
  • If you have iCloud Backup turned on for your device, the App's data is included in Apple's backup like your other device data, and Apple's Privacy Policy applies to it.
  • Data on your devices is protected by iOS and watchOS Data Protection (encryption tied to your passcode).

4. External services

The App uses Apple's MapKit to show maps. When a map is shown, map data for that area is fetched from Apple. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles information.

If you export a ride as a GPX file and share it with another app or service, that service's policies apply from then on.

5. Sharing with third parties

We hold none of your information, so we never share it with third parties.

6. Children

The App sends no one's information to us, including children's, so we never collect personal information regardless of age.

7. Deleting your information

  • Delete a ride by swiping left on it in the App's history.
  • Deleting the App deletes all ride records stored on your device.
  • Data saved to Health can be deleted in the Health app.
  • You can revoke Location and Health access at any time in the Settings app.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will announce it on this page.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy, please use GitHub Issues.
